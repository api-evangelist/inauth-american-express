# InAuth (American Express)

> InAuth is a mobile-first digital device intelligence and authentication company acquired by American Express in 2018 and now operated within American Express's fraud-management portfolio. Its technology also powers Accertify Device Intelligence. The InAuth Security Platform delivers persistent device identification, malware and criminal-tool detection, rooted/jailbroken-device detection, and transaction risk analysis, primarily through native iOS and Android SDKs embedded in merchant and financial-institution apps. Access is enterprise-gated through the Amex for Developers portal; there is no public self-serve REST API, OpenAPI specification, package-registry distribution, or InAuth-specific public code repository.

## Notes
- Capabilities: device fingerprinting, malware/root-jailbreak detection, criminal-tool detection, location and behavioral risk signals, transaction risk scoring.
- Delivery: native mobile SDKs (iOS/Android) provisioned to enterprise customers under contract; not published to public package registries.
- Related: Accertify (American Express subsidiary) — Device Intelligence powered by InAuth.
